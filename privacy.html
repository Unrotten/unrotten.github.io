Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026

About Unrotten

Unrotten is an SEC-filing research and general chat assistant, provided for informational and educational purposes only. It does not constitute financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. Always verify figures against the original SEC filing and consult a licensed professional before making financial decisions.

Information we store

  • Conversation messages and assistant replies may be stored so your chat history persists between sessions.
  • Uploaded PDF filings are stored so they can be indexed and searched, and are not knowingly shared beyond the AI providers used to answer your questions.
  • We do not knowingly collect government IDs, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. Please avoid pasting such information into the chat.

Third-party services used

  • Groq processes chat messages to generate responses.
  • SEC EDGAR, a public U.S. government system, is queried to fetch public company filings.
  • Google AdSense may display advertising on this site and may use cookies or device identifiers to personalize ads. Learn more at Google's Partner Sites policy.

Cookies & ads

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Your choices

You can clear your stored conversation history at any time from within the app. Continuing to use Unrotten after reading this page constitutes acceptance of these terms.

Disclaimer

Unrotten is a research tool. Responses are generated by AI models and may contain errors or omissions. Nothing produced by this app should be treated as professional financial, legal, or audit advice.
